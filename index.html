    Side B — Liner notes

    Records for rooms that want to move.

    놀자 (nol-ja) is Korean for “let’s play.” It’s also the whole job description.

    I build every set the way you’d sequence an album: a patient Side A that warms the room up, and a Side B that doesn’t let anyone sit back down. Weddings, birthdays, company parties, club nights — I read the room, take the requests that work, and quietly lose the ones that don’t.

    Before your event we’ll talk through the people, the must-plays and the absolutely-nots. On the night, you get a DJ who shows up early, sounds great, and keeps the floor full without ever making it about the DJ.
